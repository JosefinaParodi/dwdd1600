Our Services

At Glow Hair Salon, we offer personalized services designed to help you achieve the hair you want while keeping your hair healthy and beautiful.

Hair Color

Our hair color services are customized to complement your style and natural features. Whether you want a subtle change or a completely new color, we can create a look that is right for you.

  • All-over color
  • Root touch-ups
  • Dimensional color
  • Color transformations

Highlights

Add dimension and brightness to your hair with personalized highlights. We can create anything from soft, natural-looking highlights to brighter and more noticeable looks.

  • Full highlights
  • Partial highlights
  • Balayage
  • Face-framing highlights

Hair Extensions

Hair extensions can add length, volume, and fullness while creating a customized look. We can help you choose an extension option that works with your hair and desired style.

  • Length and volume extensions
  • Customized extension colors
  • Extension maintenance

Haircuts and Styling

Complete your look with a haircut or styling service. We can create a style that fits your preferences and works with your everyday routine.

  • Women's haircuts
  • Blowouts
  • Waves and curls
  • Special occasion styling

Preparing for Your Appointment

Come to your appointment with clean, dry hair whenever possible. If you are receiving a color or extension service, we recommend discussing your hair goals with your stylist before your appointment.
