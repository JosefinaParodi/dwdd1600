Digital Citizenship

What is Digital Citizenship?

Digital citizenship means using the internet, technology, and digital devices in a safe, responsible, respectful, and ethical way.

What is Digital Law?

Digital law refers to the rules and laws that govern how people use technology and the internet.

What is AUP?

It is a set of rules that explains how people are allowed to use an organization's technology, internet, computers, and digital resources.
